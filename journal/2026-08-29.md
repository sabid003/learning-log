Cybersecurity is one of the fastest-growing and most sensitive areas of the tech industry. This field is a world of its own, full of mystery — there is always more depth to it than what shows on the surface. I am passionate about cybersecurity, and that is why I started learning it. It has many branches, and the one I like most is ethical hacking. Ethical hacking is the process of legally breaking into a system and preparing it for any future real threat before an actual attacker gets there first. Starting with a system and taking full control of it is very thrilling for me — it feels like solving a puzzle that actually matters, not just an exercise. I look forward to getting deep into this field and building real skill in it.
